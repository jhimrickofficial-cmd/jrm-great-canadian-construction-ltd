Our proven construction & general contracting process

Every build follows a strict four-phase methodology ensuring budget compliance, safety clearance, and zero surprises at handover.

01

Initial Site Consultation

We meet on-site in Ontario or review your blueprints to define spatial needs, zoning feasibility, and realistic preliminary budget envelopes.

Duration: 1–3 Days Stage 1
02

Permits & Scope Audit

Detailed line-item estimating, engineering sign-offs, and submitting applications to Ontario municipal building departments for full clearance.

Duration: 2–4 Weeks Stage 2
03

Turnkey Construction

Active execution by our vetted trades, daily site management, safety compliance, milestone photo reporting, and proactive scheduling.

Duration: Per Schedule Stage 3
04

Final Inspection & Warranty

Thorough walkthrough, final occupancy certification from municipal inspectors, comprehensive documentation, and warranty packages.
